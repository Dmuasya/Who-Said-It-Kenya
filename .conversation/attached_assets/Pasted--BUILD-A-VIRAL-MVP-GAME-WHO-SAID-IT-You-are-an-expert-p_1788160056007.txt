# 1. THE IDEA

This is a fast-paced Kenyan political quote guessing game.

The player sees a quote attributed to a politician and must guess which politician said it from four possible answers.

Example:

> "QUOTE HERE..."

### WHO SAID IT?

🅰️ William Ruto
🅱️ Raila Odinga
🅲️ Kalonzo Musyoka
🅳️ Musalia Mudavadi

The player has only a few seconds to answer.

The faster they answer correctly, the more points they receive.

The goal is to make the game:

* funny
* addictive
* competitive
* extremely easy to understand
* extremely easy to share
* optimized for Kenyan mobile users

Think of the feeling of a combination of:

**Who Wants to Be a Millionaire + Wordle + a fast mobile trivia game + Kenyan political humor.**

But create an ORIGINAL design. Do not copy another game's interface.
